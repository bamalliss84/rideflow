# FINANCIAL AND MARKET VALIDATION — assumptions, not audited forecast
Scope: Mukalla pilot tenant economics. Future RideFlow SaaS operator revenue (D017) is not modeled here.

## Revenue
Monthly gross = paid drivers × average realized subscription. At 5,000 YER: 100=500,000; 200=1,000,000; 500=2,500,000; 1000=5,000,000 YER/month. Deduct trial/free days, discount/credits, collection leakage, refunds and taxes before net revenue. Do not equate registrations with paying drivers.

## Break-even
Paid drivers needed = fixed monthly operating costs / (average subscription collected − variable cost per paid driver). Example only: fixed 1,000,000 YER/month and contribution 4,000 YER => 250 drivers. Currency conversion for USD server/maps/SMS must use dated rate and location-specific exchange market; never combine USD and YER without conversion assumptions.

## Cost ledger to obtain quotes for
Engineering, testing devices, server/backup/monitoring, maps/geocoding, OTP/SMS, customer support, onboarding verification, payment reconciliation, sales and field recruitment, legal/licensing/insurance, refunds/credits, app store fees, fraud loss and contingencies.

## Three 12-month scenarios — template inputs
Conservative: paying drivers/month, active riders, trips per driver, churn, discount %, support staff, acquisition cost.
Base: same inputs.
Upside: same inputs.
Do not fill unsupported market numbers and present them as facts. Obtain at least 20–30 driver and 30 rider interviews and price sensitivity responses.

## Pilot KPIs and provisional gates
Bid coverage >=80%; matched-request-to-completion >=65% as stretch target; no-show <=5% initially, then <=3%; accepted-match cancellations <10%; report median ETA, driver earnings, driver retention and 30-day rider repeat. Thresholds are hypotheses to validate, not guarantees. Legal clearance required regardless of metrics.
